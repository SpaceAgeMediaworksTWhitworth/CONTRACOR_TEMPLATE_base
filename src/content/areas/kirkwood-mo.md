## Older homes need a careful hand

Kirkwood is one of the older towns in St. Louis County. Near downtown and the train station, you will find homes that are more than 100 years old. Many have steep roofs, dormers, and tall brick chimneys.

These homes were built to last. But their roofs need a roofer who knows how older homes are put together.

## Slate roofs

Some older Kirkwood homes still have slate. A good slate roof can last a very long time. When a few pieces crack or slip, the fix is usually to replace those pieces, not the whole roof.

Slate breaks if it is walked on the wrong way. We use the right gear and methods to work on it. If your slate roof is near the end, we will tell you, and we will walk you through your options.

## Steep roofs and old decks

Victorian and other older homes often have very steep roofs. They take more time and more safety gear. Under old shingles, we may find board decking instead of plywood, sometimes with gaps. We check it and fix what is needed so your new roof has a solid base.

We tell you about any extra work and cost before we do it.

## Chimneys and flashing

Old brick chimneys are one of the most common leak spots on Kirkwood homes. Flashing rusts. Mortar cracks. We replace chimney flashing when we do a roof. If a chimney needs a mason, we will tell you.

## Big trees

Kirkwood streets are lined with big, old trees. They drop leaves in gutters and limbs on roofs. We check for tree damage and clogged valleys on every inspection.

## Landmark homes

Some Kirkwood homes may have landmark status. If yours might, check with the city before you plan a new roof. We will help you figure it out first.
