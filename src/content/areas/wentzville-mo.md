## New homes, real roof problems

Wentzville is one of the fastest growing towns around. A lot of families here live in homes built in the last 20 years. It is easy to think a newer roof is safe. But that is not always true.

## Builder-grade roofs

When homes go up fast, builders often use basic shingles. That is not wrong, but those shingles are thinner. They can blow off in high winds sooner than heavier shingles.

We also see small install problems on newer roofs. Things like missing drip edge, short nails, or poor flashing. These do not always leak right away. They show up after a few years or a big storm.

If your home is newer and you have lost shingles, call us. We will look at why it happened, not just patch the spot.

## Wind with no trees to stop it

Many new subdivisions have open ground and young trees. That means wind hits roofs full force. Missing shingles and lifted ridge caps are common after a storm.

A small repair done fast keeps water out. Wait too long, and a small repair can turn into a leak inside.

## Hail and insurance

Hail storms hit this side of the county too. Hail damage is hard to see from the ground. We get on the roof, mark what we find, and take photos.

If you have real hail damage, we can help you file a claim. We meet your adjuster so they see what we saw.

## HOA approvals

Most Wentzville subdivisions have an HOA. If you need a new roof, your HOA will likely want to approve the color. We give you everything you need to send in and wait for the OK.

## Free inspection

Call us to set up a free roof check. We will tell you what we find and what, if anything, needs to be done.
