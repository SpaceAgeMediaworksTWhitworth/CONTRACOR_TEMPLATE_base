## Large homes, premium roofs

Town and Country has some of the largest homes in the St. Louis area. Many sit on big, wooded lots. Their roofs are often steep and full of angles, with dormers, turrets and more than one chimney.

Some of these homes have slate or cedar shake roofs. Others have high-end designer shingles. These roofs cost more, and they need a roofer who will take care with them.

## Slate roofs

A well-installed slate roof can last a very long time. When a few slates crack or slip, the whole roof usually does not need to go. We can replace the broken pieces and fix the flashing around them.

You should not walk on slate the wrong way. It breaks. We use the right gear and methods so we do not cause more damage than we fix.

If your slate roof is at the end of its life, we will tell you. We can talk about options, including new slate or products made to look like slate.

## Cedar shake roofs

Cedar shake looks great, but it takes more care. Over time, shakes dry out, split and grow moss. Shade from big trees can speed that up.

If your cedar roof is wearing out, you can replace it with new cedar or with a designer shingle made to look like shake. We will explain the cost and upkeep of each so you can pick.

## Complex roofs

Big roofs with many sections have more valleys, more flashing, and more places for water to get in. We check every one. When we replace a roof, we replace the flashing and underlayment too.

## Protecting your property

Large lots often have long drives, gardens and landscaping. We plan where the dumpster and materials go. We protect plants and patios. We clean up every day.
