## Room to breathe, hard to reach

Wildwood is known for its hills, woods and big lots. Babler State Park and Rockwoods Reservation are right here. Many homes sit up a long drive or on the side of a hill.

That setting is a big reason people live here. It also makes roof work harder than in a flat subdivision.

## Planning the job

Before we start a roof in Wildwood, we look at the whole lot, not just the roof. We plan:

- Where the dumpster can go without hurting the driveway
- How to get materials to the house
- How to protect trees, beds and slopes
- How to keep nails out of the yard

We use tarps and a magnet sweep to clean up. A big lot is still your yard, and we treat it that way.

## Large, complex roofs

Homes here tend to have big roofs with steep slopes, valleys and dormers. More roof means more places to check. We look at every valley, pipe boot and piece of flashing.

## Trees and hills

Tall trees near the house drop limbs in wind and ice storms. Branches that hang over the roof scrape shingles and drop leaves in the valleys. Homes on a hilltop also take more wind.

If a limb comes down on your roof, call us. We can tarp it fast to keep the rain out.

## Metal roofing

Some Wildwood homeowners choose metal. It holds up well to wind. Leaves and snow slide off. And it lasts a long time. It does cost more than shingles.

If you are thinking about it, we can price metal and shingles side by side. Then you can pick what fits your home and budget.
