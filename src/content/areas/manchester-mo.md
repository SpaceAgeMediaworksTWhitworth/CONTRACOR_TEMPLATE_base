## Ranches and split levels

Manchester has a lot of ranch and split level homes built from the 1950s to the 1980s. Many have a brick front with siding on the sides and back. They are solid homes. But by now, most have had the roof redone at least once.

## Roof and siding, one crew

On homes this age, the roof and the siding often wear out around the same time. Shingles curl and lose granules. Siding fades, cracks or warps. Trim boards start to rot.

We do both roofing and siding. If both need work, you can do them as one project. That means:

- One crew to deal with
- One schedule
- Colors that match and look good together

If only your roof needs work, we will tell you that. We will not push siding you do not need.

## Low slope add-ons

A lot of older homes here have a back room, porch or addition with a low slope roof. Regular shingles are not made for low slopes. When they are used there, water can back up and leak.

We check these areas closely. If the slope is too low for shingles, we use a roofing material made for low slopes.

## Gutters and fascia

Gutters on older homes are often worn out. They sag, leak at the seams, or pull away from the house. When water spills over, it can rot the fascia boards behind them.

We can replace gutters and fix rotted fascia when we do your roof or siding.

## Storm damage

Hail and wind can damage both your roof and your siding. After a big storm, check for dents, cracks and holes. We can inspect both and help with your insurance claim if there is real damage.
