## Our home town

Ridgeline Roofing & Exteriors is based in O'Fallon. We live here. We shop here. When a storm hits O'Fallon, it hits our homes too.

That means we know how the roofs in this town age. We know what a spring hail storm does to them. And we are close by when you need someone to come look.

## Roofs in O'Fallon subdivisions

A lot of O'Fallon homes were built in the 1990s and 2000s. Many of those roofs are now 20 years old or more. That is about when asphalt shingles start to wear out. You may see curled edges, bare spots, or granules in the gutters.

If your roof is in that age range, a storm can push it over the edge. A roof that was fine last year may need to be replaced after one bad hail storm.

## HOA rules and shingle colors

Many neighborhoods here, like WingHaven, have an HOA. Most HOAs want to approve your roof color before work starts. Some only allow certain styles.

We make this easy. We give you the exact shingle name and color to send in. We do not start until your HOA says yes. That way you do not get a letter later.

## Storm damage and insurance

Hail and high winds are the big threats here. After a storm, some damage is easy to spot, like missing shingles. Hail damage is harder to see from the ground.

We get on the roof and take photos. We show you what we found. If there is real storm damage, we can help you with your insurance claim. We meet the adjuster and make sure they see what we saw. If your roof is fine, we tell you that too.
