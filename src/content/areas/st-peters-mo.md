## Roofs that have seen a few storms

Many St. Peters homes were built in the 1970s, 80s and 90s. A lot of them have had one roof replaced already. Some are close to needing another.

A roof does not last forever. Most asphalt shingle roofs last around 20 to 25 years here, and less if they take a lot of hail. If your roof is in that range, it is a good time to have it looked at.

## Repair or replace?

This is the big question we hear. The honest answer is, it depends.

If the damage is small and the rest of the roof is in good shape, we fix it. That saves you money. If the roof is old, patched in a lot of spots, or leaking in more than one place, a new roof is often the smarter spend.

We walk you through what we see. We show you photos. Then you decide.

## What we check

When we inspect a roof in St. Peters, we look at:

- Shingles for hail hits, cracks, curling and bare spots
- Flashing around chimneys, vents and walls
- Pipe boots, which crack with age and cause leaks
- Soft spots in the roof deck
- Gutters and downspouts

## After a storm

Storms roll across St. Charles County every spring. If a storm hits your street, you may see roofers knocking on doors. Take your time. Get a real inspection and a written quote.

If there is storm damage, we can help with the insurance claim. We meet the adjuster so nothing gets missed.
