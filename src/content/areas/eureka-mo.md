## Old and new, all on the hills

Eureka sits along Interstate 44 and the Meramec River. It has an older part of town with homes that go back many years. It also has newer subdivisions built up on the hills. Six Flags is right here too.

All of that means we see every kind of roof in Eureka. Old homes with worn roofs. Newer homes with builder-grade shingles. And a lot of homes with trees all around.

## When a storm hits

Storms can come up fast here. High winds strip shingles. Limbs fall. Hail dents gutters and bruises shingles.

If your roof is open to the weather, time matters. Water can get into the attic, soak the insulation and stain the ceiling in one night.

Here is what we do:

1. **Tarp it.** We cover the damaged area to stop water from getting in.
2. **Inspect it.** Once the weather clears, we look at the whole roof and take photos.
3. **Fix it.** We repair the damage or give you a price for a new roof if it needs one.
4. **Help with the claim.** If it is storm damage, we can meet your insurance adjuster.

## Hilly lots and trees

Many homes in Eureka sit on hills. Hilltop homes catch more wind. Homes lower down often have big trees close to the house. Both can be hard on a roof.

We look for shingles lifted by wind, scrapes from branches, and valleys full of leaves. We also check gutters, since they fill up fast here.

## Older homes near downtown

Older homes can have more than one layer of shingles. The wood under them may need work. When we replace one of these roofs, we tear off down to the deck and fix what needs fixing. We tell you the cost first.
