## Big homes, big roofs

Many homes in Chesterfield are larger. Their roofs are steep and have lots of parts. Valleys, dormers, skylights and chimneys all come together. That looks great from the street. But every one of those spots is a place where a leak can start.

## What makes a complex roof harder

A simple roof has two sides and a ridge. A complex roof might have ten or more sections. Here is what that means:

- **More valleys.** Water from two slopes runs into one channel. Valleys wear out first.
- **More flashing.** Every wall, dormer and chimney needs metal flashing. If it is done wrong, water gets in.
- **Steep pitches.** Steep roofs take more safety gear and more time.
- **Skylights.** These are great until the seal fails.

We take our time on these roofs. We check every valley and every piece of flashing. When we replace a roof, we replace the flashing too, not just the shingles.

## Premium roofing

Some homes in Chesterfield have cedar shake or other high-end roofs. If you have one, we can inspect it and tell you what shape it is in. If it is time to replace, we can talk about your options, including designer shingles that look like shake.

## Commercial roofs in the Valley

Chesterfield Valley is full of shops, offices and warehouses. Many of those have flat or low slope roofs. These roofs have their own problems. Water can pond. Seams can split. Drains can clog.

We inspect flat roofs, find leaks, and repair or replace them. We give you a clear report so you can plan your budget.

## Storm damage

Hail and high wind are common here. After a storm, we can inspect your roof and help with the insurance claim if there is real damage.
