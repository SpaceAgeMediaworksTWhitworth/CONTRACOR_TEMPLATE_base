## Old roofs and new roofs

St. Charles is a town of two halves. Near Main Street and in Frenchtown, some homes have stood for over 100 years. Out west and in New Town, many homes are only a few years old.

We work on both. But old homes need a different kind of care, so we want to explain how we handle them.

## Working on older St. Charles homes

Old homes can hide problems. We often find more than one layer of shingles. Sometimes the roof boards under them are soft or rotted. Chimneys may need new flashing. Steep roofs and tight lots near the street take more setup.

We tear off to the bare deck so we can see what is there. If boards are bad, we show you photos and tell you the cost before we fix them. No surprises on your bill.

## Historic district rules

Parts of St. Charles are historic districts. Some of these areas review exterior changes like roofing. Rules can cover color and material.

We do not guess. Before we start, we check with the city and help you get any approval you need. It is better to take a few extra days than to redo a roof.

## Trees, gutters and storms

A lot of older streets have big trees. Limbs can scrape shingles or fall in a storm. Leaves also clog gutters, and clogged gutters can push water under the roof edge.

We look at your gutters when we look at your roof. If they need cleaning, repair or new guards, we will tell you.

## Newer homes in St. Charles

For newer homes, the main risks are hail and wind. We do full inspections, fix storm damage, and help with insurance claims if you need it.
