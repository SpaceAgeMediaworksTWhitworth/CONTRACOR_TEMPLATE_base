## Newer homes, HOA rules

Most homes in Dardenne Prairie are in newer subdivisions like Barathaven. Most of those subdivisions have an HOA. That changes how a roof job works.

Before a new roof goes on, your HOA may want to see the shingle brand, style and color. Some have a short list of colors you can pick from. Some want all homes on the street to match.

## How we handle HOA approval

We make this part simple for you:

1. We help you pick a shingle that fits your HOA rules.
2. We give you the exact product name and color to submit.
3. We wait for your HOA to say yes.
4. Then we set the install date.

No guessing. No letters from the HOA after the job is done.

## When a storm hits your street

After a big hail storm, it can feel like every house on the street is getting a new roof. Signs pop up in yards. Roofers knock on doors.

It is smart to slow down. Hail damage is real, but not every roof gets hit the same way. We get on your roof, mark each hit, and take photos. Then we tell you if it is enough to file a claim.

## Help with insurance claims

If you do have storm damage, a claim can feel like a lot. We help. We meet your adjuster on the roof and point out what we found. We explain what your policy covers in plain words, based on what the insurance company sends you.

You still make every choice. We just help you get a fair look.

## Roofs reaching 20 years

Many homes here were built in the late 1990s and 2000s. Those first roofs are getting close to the end. If you see worn spots, curled shingles or granules in your gutters, it may be time.
