## A small town with a mix of homes

Cottleville is a small city, but it has a little of everything. There are older homes near Old Town. There are newer subdivisions built in the 1990s and 2000s. And St. Charles Community College sits right in town.

No matter which kind of home you have, the roof has the same job. Keep the water out. When it stops doing that, we can help.

## Finding the real leak

A leak is the most common reason people call us. Water stains on the ceiling. A drip in the attic. A damp spot around a light.

The tricky part is that water travels. The stain is often not right under the problem. We trace it back to where it gets in. Most of the time it is one of these:

- A cracked rubber boot around a vent pipe
- Loose or rusted flashing at a chimney or wall
- Missing or cracked shingles
- A valley that has worn out

If the rest of your roof is solid, we fix the one spot. No need to sell you a whole roof.

## When it is time to replace

Many homes here are now 20 years old or more. That puts a lot of original roofs near the end of their life. If you have more than one leak, lots of worn shingles, or hail damage, a new roof may be the better choice.

We will tell you which one makes sense. We show you photos so you can see it too.

## Gutters matter

Gutters move water off your roof and away from your house. When they clog or pull loose, water backs up under the roof edge. That can rot the wood.

We check gutters on every roof inspection. We can clean, fix or replace them.
