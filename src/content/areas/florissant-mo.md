## The Florissant ranch

Florissant grew fast after World War II. Lots of ranch homes went up in the 1950s and 1960s. Many have brick fronts, simple roof lines and one level.

These homes are easy to love. And their simple roofs are some of the easiest roofs to replace. That can help keep the cost reasonable.

## What we check on a ranch roof

Even simple roofs need a careful look. On Florissant ranches, we check:

- The shingles for age, hail hits and wear
- The wood deck under the shingles for soft spots
- Pipe boots and roof vents
- Chimney flashing, if you have a chimney
- Attic venting and airflow
- Gutters and downspouts

Many homes this age were built with little attic venting. Poor airflow traps heat and moisture. That wears out shingles faster. When we replace a roof, we look at your vents and suggest fixes if needed.

## Tear off, do it right

Some older roofs have a second layer of shingles laid over the first. We do not do that. We tear off to the wood. That way we can see and fix any bad boards. The new roof lasts longer and sits flat.

## Gutters

Older gutters sag, leak and pull away from the house. When they overflow, water can soak the fascia and run down the foundation. We can replace gutters when we do your roof or as a separate job.

## Old Town Florissant

Old Town Florissant is one of the oldest parts of the St. Louis area. The Old St. Ferdinand Shrine is here. Homes in this part of town are much older than the ranches, and there may be extra review for exterior work. Check with the city first. We are glad to help.
