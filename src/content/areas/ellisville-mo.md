## Catch it early

Most roof problems start small. A cracked pipe boot. A few loose shingles. A bit of flashing that pulled away. Left alone, a small problem turns into a leak. A leak turns into wet insulation, stained ceilings and rotted wood.

In Ellisville, many homes are 30 to 50 years old. Most have had a newer roof put on at some point. Keeping an eye on that roof is the best way to make it last.

## What a roof inspection covers

When we inspect a roof in Ellisville, we check:

- Shingles for cracks, curling, bare spots and hail hits
- Flashing at chimneys, walls and skylights
- Rubber boots around vent pipes
- Ridge caps and roof vents
- Gutters and downspouts
- The attic, if we can get in, for stains and airflow

We take photos of anything we find. We explain it in plain words. Then we give you a written price for any work.

## Small repairs, done right

A lot of what we do in Ellisville is repair. Pipe boots wear out. Flashing loosens. Storms knock off a few shingles. These fixes are quick and cost far less than a new roof.

If your roof only needs a repair, that is what we will recommend.

## After a storm

Hail and wind hit West County often. After a storm, check for shingles in the yard or dents on gutters and vents. Those are signs your roof may have been hit.

We can inspect it and tell you if the damage is real. If it is, we can help you with the insurance claim.

## When it is time for a new roof

If your roof is old, leaking in more than one spot, or badly hail damaged, replacing it may cost less over time than patching it again and again. We will tell you when that point comes.
