## Mid-century homes

A lot of Creve Coeur homes went up in the 1950s, 60s and 70s. Many are long ranch homes with wide, low roofs. Others are two-story homes on wooded lots. There are also newer and larger homes mixed in.

These homes are well built. But older roof designs and big old trees mean we see some of the same problems again and again.

## Low slope ranch roofs

Many mid-century ranches have roofs with a gentle slope. They look sleek and simple. But low slopes shed water slower. Wind-driven rain and ice can back up under shingles more easily.

When we replace a low slope roof, we check the pitch first. If it is low, we use extra underlayment. On very low sections, like a porch or carport, we use a roofing material made for low slopes. That stops leaks before they start.

## Big trees, busy gutters

Creve Coeur has a lot of tall, older trees. They shade the house, but they also:

- Drop leaves that clog gutters and valleys
- Rub shingles with low branches
- Drop limbs on the roof in wind and ice storms
- Keep roofs damp, which invites moss

We check for all of these on every inspection. We can clean, repair or replace gutters, and we can add gutter guards.

## Chimneys and flashing

Many older homes here have brick chimneys. The flashing where the roof meets the chimney is a common leak spot. Old flashing rusts or pulls loose. Mortar cracks.

When we do a roof, we replace chimney flashing too. If we see chimney issues that need a mason, we will tell you.

## Storm damage

Hail and wind storms can damage roofs here. After a big storm, we can inspect your roof and help with the insurance claim if there is real damage.
