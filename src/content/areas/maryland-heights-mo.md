## Homes and businesses

Maryland Heights is a busy place. There are streets of houses, lots of apartments, and a large business area along Interstate 270 and Page Avenue. Westport Plaza and Creve Coeur Lake Park are here too.

That means we see a lot of different roofs in this town. Pitched shingle roofs on houses. Flat and low slope roofs on offices, warehouses and stores.

## Flat roofs on commercial buildings

Flat roofs are not really flat. They are built with a small slope so water runs to drains. When that works, they do fine. When it does not, problems start.

Here is what we see most on flat commercial roofs:

- **Ponding water.** Water sits after rain and wears the roof down.
- **Clogged drains.** Leaves and trash block the drains and scuppers.
- **Split seams.** Seams between roof sheets pull apart over time.
- **Worn flashing.** Flashing around rooftop units, pipes and walls lets water in.
- **Punctures.** Foot traffic and dropped tools can poke holes.

We inspect the whole roof and give you a clear report with photos. Then we tell you what needs to be fixed now and what can wait. That helps you plan your budget.
