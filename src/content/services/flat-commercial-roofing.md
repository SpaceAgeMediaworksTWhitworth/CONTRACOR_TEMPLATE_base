Flat roofs work differently than sloped ones. Water doesn't run off as fast, so small problems turn into leaks quicker. Ridgeline Roofing & Exteriors repairs and replaces flat and low-slope roofs for homeowners and small businesses in O'Fallon and the St. Louis area.

## Where you'll find flat roofs

- Home additions, porches, and garages
- Shops, warehouses, and pole barns
- Strip centers and small offices
- Churches and older buildings

"Flat" roofs are not totally flat. They have a slight slope so water drains to the edges or to drains. When that slope or drain stops working, trouble starts.

## Signs your flat roof needs help

- Water that sits for days after rain
- Bubbles or blisters in the surface
- Cracks, splits, or worn spots
- Seams lifting at the edges
- Rust or gaps around rooftop units
- Stains on ceilings or wet ceiling tiles
- Plants or moss growing in low spots

## Common flat roof materials

**TPO.** A white or light-colored sheet that bounces back sunlight. The seams are welded with heat. Common on newer commercial buildings.

**EPDM (rubber roofing).** A black rubber sheet. It's been around a long time and holds up well to cold weather.

**Modified bitumen.** Layers of asphalt-based material, often used on homes and smaller buildings. Tough and good with foot traffic.

**Roof coatings.** A liquid coating rolled or sprayed over an existing roof that is still in decent shape. It can seal small problems and add life. It won't fix a roof with lots of wet insulation.

We'll tell you which option fits your building and budget.

## Repair, coat, or replace?

- **Repair** if the problem is in a few spots and the rest of the roof is solid.
- **Coat** if the roof is aging but mostly dry and sound.
- **Replace** if the roof is failing in many places or the insulation under it is soaked.

Wet insulation is a big deal on flat roofs. It adds weight, doesn't insulate, and can rot the deck. We check for it before we recommend anything.

## Midwest weather and flat roofs

Our weather is tough on flat roofs. Hot summers break down the surface. Heavy rain tests the drains. Snow adds weight. Freeze and thaw cycles work water into small cracks and make them bigger. Keeping drains and gutters clear goes a long way.

## What affects the price

- The size of the roof
- The material you choose
- Whether old layers need to come off
- How much wet insulation needs replacing
- Drains, rooftop units, and other things to work around

## How long it takes

Small repairs can take a few hours. A new roof on a small building may take a few days. Larger buildings take longer. Weather plays a part, since most flat roof materials need dry conditions to go on right.

## Keep your flat roof in good shape

- Clear drains and gutters, especially in fall
- Have it checked once or twice a year
- Fix small problems right away
- Keep foot traffic to a minimum
