Most people never look at their roof until something goes wrong. By then, a small fix may have turned into a big one. A roof inspection from Ridgeline Roofing & Exteriors tells you where your roof stands so you can plan instead of react.

## Why get a roof inspection

Roofs wear out slowly. You can't see most problems from the ground. An inspection helps you:

- Catch small problems before they turn into leaks
- Know how much life your roof has left
- Find storm damage you didn't know about
- Plan and budget for a new roof before you're forced into it
- Get honest facts when buying or selling a home

## When to get one

- **Every year.** Fall is a great time, before winter weather hits.
- **After a storm.** Hail and wind can damage a roof without leaving obvious signs.
- **When buying a home.** Know what you're getting before you close.
- **Before selling.** Fix small things up front so they don't slow down the sale.
- **When your roof hits 15 years or so.** That's when wear starts to show on many shingle roofs.

## What we check

**On the roof:**

- Shingles for cracks, curling, missing pieces, and worn grit
- Signs of hail or wind damage
- Flashing around chimneys, walls, and skylights
- Pipe boots and vents
- Ridge caps and roof edges
- Soft spots in the roof deck

**In the attic (when we can get in):**

- Water stains on the wood
- Mold or musty smells
- Daylight coming through
- Airflow and vents
- Signs of heat or moisture build-up

**Around the house:**

- Gutters and downspouts
- Fascia and soffit
- Grit washing out at the downspouts

In the St. Louis area, we pay close attention to the things our weather is hard on. Hail. Freeze and thaw cycles. Ice dams at the roof edge. Summer heat that bakes shingles from both sides when attic airflow is poor.

## What you get

We take photos of what we find. Then we walk you through it in plain words. We'll sort it into three groups:

- **Needs work now.** Things that can let water in soon.
- **Keep an eye on it.** Wear that isn't urgent but will need attention.
- **Looks good.** The parts that are in fine shape.

We won't pad the list. If your roof is fine, we'll say so.

## What affects the cost

Some roofers offer free inspections, and some charge, especially for detailed reports for home sales. The price, if any, depends on the size of the roof, how steep it is, and how detailed a report you need. Ask us when you call.

## How long it takes

Most inspections take under an hour. You don't have to be home, but if you are, we're happy to show you photos right there.

## How to pick an inspector

Look for someone who actually gets on the roof, not just someone looking from the yard with binoculars. Ask if they check the attic. Ask to see photos. And be careful with anyone who finds a big problem on every roof they look at.
