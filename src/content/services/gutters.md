Gutters don't get much attention until they fail. But they do a big job. They catch the water off your roof and move it away from your house. When they stop working, water ends up where it shouldn't. Ridgeline Roofing & Exteriors installs and repairs gutters for homes in O'Fallon and the St. Louis area.

## Why gutters matter

When gutters clog, sag, or leak, water goes places that cause real damage:

- **Roof edge.** Water backs up under shingles and rots the wood.
- **Fascia and soffit.** The boards behind the gutters soak up water and rot.
- **Siding.** Water runs down the walls and stains or damages siding.
- **Foundation.** Water pools next to the house and can lead to cracks and wet basements.
- **Landscaping.** Water spills over and washes out beds and mulch.

In winter, clogged gutters also help cause ice dams. Water freezes at the roof edge and pushes under the shingles.

## Signs you need gutter work

- Water pours over the edge in heavy rain
- Gutters sag or pull away from the house
- Drips at seams or corners
- Rust, holes, or cracks
- Stains on siding under the gutters
- Water pooling by the foundation
- Plants growing in the gutters

## Gutter options

**Seamless aluminum.** The most common choice. We form each run on site from one piece, so there are fewer seams to drip. Comes in lots of colors.

**Gutter sizes.** Standard 5-inch gutters work on many homes. Larger 6-inch gutters handle more water. They're a good fit for big or steep roofs or homes that see a lot of runoff.

**Downspouts.** Bigger downspouts clog less and move water faster. Where they drain matters too. We'll aim them away from your foundation.

## Gutter guards

Guards cover the top of the gutter to keep leaves out. There are a few types, like screens and solid covers. They help most if you have trees near the house. They cut down on cleanings, but no guard is perfect. Plan on a quick check now and then.

## Repairs we do

- Resealing leaky seams and corners
- Re-hanging sagging gutters
- Fixing the slope so water flows to the downspout
- Replacing damaged sections
- Fixing rotted fascia boards
- Adding or moving downspouts

## How long it takes

Most gutter jobs take a day or less. If we find rotted boards behind the old gutters, that can add some time. We'll show you before we fix it.

## Doing gutters with a new roof

If you're getting a new roof, it can be smart to do gutters at the same time. The roof edge and gutters work together. Doing both at once means one crew, one mess, and a better fit.

## How to pick a gutter installer

Ask if they make seamless gutters on site. Ask how they handle slope and downspout placement. Check that they look at the boards behind the gutters, not just the gutters.
