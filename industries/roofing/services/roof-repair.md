## Signs your roof needs a repair

Some problems are easy to spot from the ground. Others hide in the attic. Watch for:

- Brown or yellow stains on ceilings
- Peeling paint near the top of walls
- Drips or damp spots in the attic after rain
- Shingles missing, lifted, or lying in the yard
- Rusty or bent metal around the chimney or vents
- A musty smell upstairs

If you see any of these, don't wait for the next big storm.

## What usually goes wrong

Most roof problems come from a short list:

- **Missing or lifted shingles.** Wind gets under them and pulls them loose.
- **Flashing.** This is the metal that seals around chimneys, walls, and skylights. It can rust, pull away, or lose its seal.
- **Pipe boots.** The rubber around vent pipes cracks from sun and cold.
- **Nail pops.** Nails push up over time and poke holes in the shingle above.
- **Ice dams.** In winter, ice builds up at the edge of the roof and pushes water under the shingles.
- **Clogged gutters.** Water backs up under the roof edge and rots the wood.

Here's the tricky part. Water runs along boards before it drips. The stain on your ceiling may be several feet from the real problem. That's why we check the attic too.

## Repair options

It depends on what we find:

- Replacing a few shingles
- Resealing or replacing flashing
- Swapping out cracked pipe boots
- Fixing a small section of rotted wood
- Fixing or adding vents

We try to match your shingles as close as we can. If your roof is older, the color may look a bit different at first. It often blends in over time.

## What affects the price

Every repair is different. The cost depends on:

Most repairs take a few hours. Bigger ones, like a section of rotted wood, can take a day. If it's raining when you call, we can put a tarp on first and come back to make the real fix when it's dry.

We'd rather fix your roof than sell you a new one you don't need. But if your roof is old and failing in many spots, patching it is money you won't get back. We'll show you photos and give you our honest take. Then it's your call.
