Your siding is your home's raincoat. It keeps water, wind, and bugs out of your walls. When it's cracked, loose, or damaged by hail, water can get behind it and cause problems you can't see. {{business}} repairs and installs siding for homes in {{city}} and the {{region}}.

## Signs your siding needs work

Walk around your house and look for:

- Cracks, holes, or broken pieces
- Dents or chips from hail
- Pieces that are loose, warped, or bulging
- Gaps at corners or around windows
- Faded color or a chalky film
- Mold, mildew, or stains that keep coming back
- Soft spots when you press on it

Inside, watch for peeling paint or wallpaper on outside walls. That can be a sign water is getting in.

## Siding materials

**Vinyl siding.** The most common choice. It costs less, comes in lots of colors, and needs very little care. Thicker vinyl holds up better to bumps and hail.

**Fiber cement.** Made from cement and wood fibers. It's heavy, stands up well to weather and bugs, and looks like painted wood. It costs more and takes longer to put on. It may need a fresh coat of paint down the road.

**Engineered wood.** Wood fibers pressed and treated to hold up to weather. It has a real wood look and is lighter than fiber cement.

We'll show you samples and help you pick what fits your home and budget.

## Storm damage to siding

Hail storms in the {{region}} don't just hit roofs. They crack vinyl, chip paint, and dent metal trim. Wind can pull siding loose. If your roof got hit, check your siding too.

Take photos of any damage. Many insurance policies cover storm damage to siding. Your claim is between you and your insurer, but we can check the damage and share photos to help you understand what's there.

## Repair or replace?

- **Repair** works when the damage is in a few spots and we can get a close color match.
- **Replace** makes sense when damage is widespread, the siding is old and faded, or there's rot underneath.

Matching older siding can be hard. Colors fade and styles change. We'll be honest about how close we can get.

## What's under the siding matters

Siding is only part of the wall. Under it is house wrap, which helps keep water out, and the wood sheathing. When we take off old siding, we check for rot and fix it before the new siding goes on. If we find problems, we'll show you photos and tell you the cost first.

## What affects the price

- The size of your home
- The siding material and style
- How much old siding comes off
- Rotted wood that needs to be fixed
- Trim, corners, and work around windows and doors
- How many stories tall your home is

You'll get a written quote that spells it out.

## How long it takes

Small repairs can be done in a few hours. A full home often takes several days to a couple of weeks. Weather and the material you pick play a part.

## What to expect

We protect your plants and yard, take off the old siding, fix any bad wood, and put up house wrap. Then the new siding and trim go on. We clean up each day and walk the finished job with you.

Ask to see photos of past work. Ask what goes under the siding, not just what goes on top. Get the materials and price in writing.
