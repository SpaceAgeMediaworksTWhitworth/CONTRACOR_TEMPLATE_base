## What storm damage looks like

Some damage is easy to see. A missing chunk of shingles or a branch on the roof is hard to miss. Hail damage is sneakier.

**Hail damage signs:**

- Dark spots or soft bruises on shingles
- Grit washed into gutters or at the bottom of downspouts
- Dents on gutters, vents, and metal flashing
- Cracked or split shingles
- Dings on siding, window frames, or your car

**Wind damage signs:**

- Missing shingles
- Shingles that are lifted, creased, or flipped up
- Loose flashing or ridge caps
- Debris on the roof

If your neighbors are getting roof work after a storm, it's worth having yours checked too.

## Why you shouldn't wait

Hail damage doesn't always leak right away. When hail knocks the grit off a shingle, the sun starts to break it down faster. Cracks let water in a little at a time. By the time you see a stain, the wood may already be wet.

Many insurance policies also set a time limit for reporting storm damage. Check yours so you don't miss it.

## Repair or full replacement?

It depends on how much damage there is and how old your roof is. A few damaged shingles can usually be repaired. If hail hit the whole roof, a patch won't hold up for long. We'll show you what we see and explain your options.

## What affects the cost

- How much of the roof was damaged
- Whether water got into the wood or attic
- The type of roof and materials
- Whether gutters, siding, or vents also need work

If you have an insurance claim, what your policy covers is up to your insurance company. We can help you understand the damage so you can have that talk with them.

## Watch out for storm chasers

After big storms, out-of-town crews show up and knock on doors. Some do fine work. Others take a deposit and disappear, or leave you with problems when they're long gone. Be careful with anyone who:

- Pushes you to sign that day
- Asks for a big payment up front
- Doesn't have a local address or phone number
- Offers to cover your deductible

A local roofer will still be around if you have a question next year.
